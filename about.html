Built by a Therapist,
For Therapists.

"As a psychotherapist specializing in trauma work, I found myself frustrated by EMDR and Brainspotting tools that were either prohibitively expensive or felt stuck in the past. I realized that most telehealth trauma software is built by developers interpreting a therapist's needs, rather than someone who actually sits in the chair. I built Bright Eyed Therapist to bridge that gap—creating the modern, intuitive, and affordable trauma telehealth tools I wished I had in my own practice."

Our Mission

I wanted to make clean, lightweight tools for trauma processing and case conceptualization—EMDR bilateral stimulation, Brainspotting gaze scanning, trauma timelines—that were easy to use, easy to access, and most importantly—inexpensive but useful enough that it feels like a steal.

At Bright Eyed Therapist, the goal is to offer tools that feel as grounded and thoughtful as the trauma work you do in session: no bloated dashboards, no distracting "features for the sake of features"— just focused, well-crafted utilities that support real clinical work with trauma clients.

Community Driven

The early access beta is intentionally small so I can listen closely. Every feature request, friction point, or “this would make my life easier” email directly shapes the roadmap.

Over time, the suite will grow into a focused set of tools that reflect the realities of contemporary practice—remote work, flexible scheduling, and clients who expect experiences that feel as polished as the rest of their digital life.

What's Available Now

The beta includes five focused tools for trauma telehealth work:

  • EMDR Suite — Free bilateral stimulation with shareable client links
  • EMDR Guided — Protocol prompts (notes stay in your browser)
  • Brainspotting — Guided gaze scanning with biolateral audio
  • Life Map — Visual timeline for trauma history and resources
  • Genogram Genie — Family pattern mapping for case conceptualization

Each tool is actively being refined based on clinician feedback. If something feels off or you have ideas, I genuinely want to hear about it.

Practice-Informed Craft

Every tool starts at the whiteboard: what actually happens in trauma processing, and how can software stay out of the way of activation, pacing, and safety. From there, I design interactions to be predictable, calm, and easy to explain to clients in a sentence or two.

My hope is that Bright Eyed Therapist becomes a quiet, reliable part of your trauma telehealth workflow—letting you spend less energy on tech, and more on the work that really matters.
