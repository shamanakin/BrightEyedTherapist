Terms of Use

1. Data Privacy

  • Your notes are sent directly to your chosen AI provider (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or xAI)
  • BrightEyedTherapist is not intended to store these note contents as part of a permanent chart
  • Your API key is stored only in YOUR browser's local storage
  • We recommend reviewing your AI provider's data handling policies

2. Professional Responsibility

  • AI-generated notes are drafts only
  • YOU are responsible for reviewing and editing all output
  • YOU are responsible for clinical accuracy of final documentation
  • This tool does not replace professional clinical judgment

  • This tool is intended for licensed mental health professionals only

  • Ensure compliance with your jurisdiction's documentation requirements

  • Requires adding a payment method to the AI provider
  • Typical cost: $0.01-0.05 per note generated
  • You're billed directly by the AI provider
