Built by a Therapist,
For Therapists.

"As a psychotherapist, I found myself frustrated by tools that were either prohibitively expensive or felt stuck in the past. I realized that most clinical software is built by developers interpreting a therapist’s needs, rather than someone who actually sits in the chair. I built Bright Eyed Therapist to bridge that gap—creating the modern, intuitive, and affordable tools I wished I had in my own practice."

Our Mission

I wanted to make clean, lightweight apps to replace commonly used tools that were easy to use, easy to access, and most importantly—inexpensive but useful enough that it feels like a steal.

At Bright Eyed Therapist, the goal is to offer tools that feel as grounded and thoughtful as the work you do in session: no bloated dashboards, no distracting “features for the sake of features”— just focused, well-crafted utilities that support real clinical work.

Community Driven

The early access beta is intentionally small so I can listen closely. Every feature request, friction point, or “this would make my life easier” email directly shapes the roadmap.

Over time, the suite will grow into a focused set of tools that reflect the realities of contemporary practice—remote work, flexible scheduling, and clients who expect experiences that feel as polished as the rest of their digital life.

Practice-Informed Craft

Every tool starts at the whiteboard: what actually happens in the room, and how can software stay out of the way of attunement, pacing, and safety. From there, I design interactions to be predictable, calm, and easy to explain to clients in a sentence or two.

My hope is that Bright Eyed Therapist becomes a quiet, reliable part of your workflow—letting you spend less energy on tech, and more on the work that really matters.
