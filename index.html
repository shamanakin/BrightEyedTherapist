Frequently Asked Questions

Is the EMDR tool really free?

Yes. Full bilateral stimulation with share-to-client links, adjustable speed, color, direction, audio tones, and session tracking. Free forever. No trial period, no credit card, no catch.

What does "Founding Member" mean?

Founding Members get access to every tool in the suite — now and in the future — for $5/month. This is earlybird launch pricing that will increase as more tools are added. Your rate is locked in at $5 forever.

Is this HIPAA compliant? Do you offer a BAA?

Bright Eyed Therapist is designed to minimize server-side clinical content, but your own compliance review still matters. Core visual tools primarily store content in your browser, while account, billing, and support data remain on our servers. A BAA is not included by default.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel your Founding Member subscription anytime from your account page. You keep your free EMDR access regardless. No contracts, no penalties.

What makes this different from other EMDR tools?

Built by a practicing therapist, not a dev shop. Free EMDR (competitors charge $15–20/mo). A local-first approach for the core visual tools. Modern, clean interface. And Brainspotting + case conceptualization tools bundled at $5/mo — not $20+.
