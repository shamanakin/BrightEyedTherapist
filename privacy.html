Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 20, 2025

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Bright Eyed Therapist (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, web applications, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service. If you do not agree, you must not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1. Information You Provide

  • Account Information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), and basic profile details.
  • Subscription and Billing Information. If you subscribe to a paid plan, our payment processor (such as Stripe) collects payment information (e.g., card details, billing address). We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Communications. If you contact us via email or through a contact form, we collect the information you provide (such as your name, email, and message content).

2.2. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data. We may collect information about how you access and use the Service, such as pages viewed, apps opened (EMDR Suite, EMDR Guided, Genogram Genie, Life Map), timestamps, and basic interaction events. This helps us understand performance and improve the tools.
  • Device and Log Data. Our servers may log information such as IP address, browser type, operating system, and the date and time of requests.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies. We may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain your session, remember preferences, and support basic analytics.

In practice, the data we routinely keep server-side is primarily account, subscription, payment, support, and operational metadata rather than full clinical records.

2.3. Data Stored Locally in the Browser

Some aspects of the tools intentionally store data only in your browser, rather than on our servers. For example:

  • Genogram Genie may store genogram data in local browser storage on the device you are using, visible only in that browser profile unless you export/import it.
  • The Life Map may store visual or theme preferences in local storage.

This information is not transmitted to our servers by default. Clearing your browser data or using a different device/profile will remove or isolate this local information.

3. Clinical Data and PHI

The Service is designed with the expectation that it will be used by trained Clinicians as a tool to support their work, not as a full electronic health record (EHR). We do not intend for the platform to store or manage complete clinical records or large volumes of protected health information (PHI).

In particular:

  • The EMDR Suite, EMDR Guided, Genogram Genie, and Life Map are primarily in-session visualization and conceptual tools. Clinical content entered into these tools is not persistently stored on our servers by default.
  • Any identifiers (such as client names or initials) that you enter into a genogram or timeline are under your control. You should minimize identifying details where possible and follow your own organizational and legal requirements regarding PHI.
  • We may collect limited metadata about app usage (for example, which tool was used and when) for analytics purposes, but this metadata is not intended to include client-identifying content.
  • Note Genie follows a different model: content entered into that tool is sent to the third-party AI provider you select so the provider can generate draft output.

You, as the Clinician, are solely responsible for determining whether and how to use the Service in a way that complies with HIPAA and any other privacy or data protection regulations applicable to your practice. Use of the Service does not, by itself, create a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) between you and Bright Eyed Therapist.

If you use Note Genie, you should review the privacy, retention, and training policies of the AI provider you choose before entering any sensitive material.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service and its features.
  • To authenticate users, manage accounts, and enforce access controls and subscriptions.
  • To process payments and manage billing through third-party processors.
  • To respond to inquiries, provide support, and communicate about updates or changes.
  • To monitor usage patterns, troubleshoot issues, and enhance performance and reliability.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the Service and comply with legal obligations.

5. Legal Basis (if applicable)

If you are located in a region that requires a legal basis for processing personal data (such as the European Economic Area), our legal bases may include:

  • Performance of a contract (providing the Service you sign up for).
  • Legitimate interests (such as improving the Service and ensuring security).
  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Consent, where required (for example, for specific communications or cookies).

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following limited ways:

  • Service Providers. With trusted third parties who perform services on our behalf, such as payment processing (Stripe), email delivery, or hosting. These providers are authorized to use your information only as necessary to provide services to us.
  • Analytics and Monitoring. With tools or services that help us understand performance and usage, in aggregate or with minimized identifiers where possible.
  • Legal Requirements. If required by law, subpoena, or court order, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.
  • Business Transfers. In connection with any merger, sale of assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and subscription information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Analytics and log data may be retained for shorter or longer periods depending on our operational needs and applicable regulations. Locally stored data in the browser (such as genograms saved in local storage) is controlled by you and your device settings.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including secure connections (HTTPS) and hashed passwords. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and for any activity under your account. If you suspect unauthorized access, please contact us promptly.

9. International Transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located in the United States and other jurisdictions. By using the Service, you consent to the transfer of your information to these locations, which may have different data protection laws than your jurisdiction.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise these rights, you can contact us at the email address below. We may ask you to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

11. Children’s Privacy

The Service is intended for use by Clinicians, not by children or therapy clients directly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
