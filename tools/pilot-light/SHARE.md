# Pilot Light: a one-minute check-in for your drive

*Shared via Bright Eyed Therapist*

Some days the get-up-and-go is there; some days it's just the pilot light. **Pilot Light** helps you notice which days are which, and what tends to come before the better ones. There's no account, no app store and no one watching.

---

### Start in 60 seconds

1. **Open `index.html`** from the folder you were sent (or the link, if someone hosted it).
2. **Slide "Drive today"** from 0 to 10. Go with your gut. That alone is a complete day.
3. If you like, **tick a few things**: sleep, the levers you pulled (morning light, moving, eating, connecting, winding down), a small win.
4. Close the tab. It saves automatically.

Come back tomorrow. After a week or two, the **heatmap** fills in and **Patterns** starts showing things like *"average drive 6.4 on days with morning light vs 4.1 without."*

### Make it yours

- **Settings → What to track**: turn sections on or off. Try *Minimal* if you only want the slider.
- **Settings → Your lists**: rename or add levers ("cold shower", "journaled"), scales ("anxiety", "craving"), meds or supplements, or any habit you want to keep an eye on. Nothing personal is preloaded.
- **Light or dark**, your **time zone**, and whether the week starts on Sunday or Monday.

### Tips if your brain likes to wander

- Keep the tab pinned, or bookmark it on your phone's home screen.
- Keyboard: `0`–`9` sets drive, `←` `→` moves between days, `T` jumps to today.
- Missed days don't break anything. Skip freely, and back-fill if you want.
- Try it with **example days** first (on the welcome card) to see what a few weeks look like, then remove them in one click.

### Private by design

- Everything stays **in your browser, on your device**. Pilot Light makes no network requests: no analytics, no cookies, no server.
- **Export** now and then to keep a backup file (clearing browser data wipes it). **Import** moves it to another device.
- If you share your *setup* with a friend, only your choices and checklists go along. Your days and notes never do.

### Seeing someone about this?

Turn on **For my next session**, and use **Print / PDF summary** (under Patterns) for a one-page picture of the last 30 days. You decide what, if anything, to share.
