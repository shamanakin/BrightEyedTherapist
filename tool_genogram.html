Genogram Key

Symbols:
  • Square: Male
  • Circle: Female
  • Diamond: Other / Nonbinary
  • 'X' inside shape: Deceased
Clinical Indicators (Shaded Quadrants):
  • Top-Left: Trauma / Suspected Trauma
  • Top-Right: Mental Health (Confirmed / Suspected)
  • Bottom-Left: Substance Use
  • Bottom-Right: Physical Ailment
Emotional Lines:
  • Thin dashed line: Distant
  • Two parallel lines: Close
  • Three parallel lines: Enmeshed
  • Zigzag: Hostile
  • Close lines + zigzag: Volatile
  • Solid line with break + bars: Cut-off
  • Jagged line + arrow: Abuse
  • P / S / E badge: Physical / Sexual / Emotional subtype
Structural Lines:
  • Solid line: Married
  • Dashed line: Partner (Unmarried)
  • Solid line with single slash: Separated
  • Solid line with double slash: Divorced
